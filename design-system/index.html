Overview

Madison is one product for a bank's second line: regulatory change, assurance (control testing) and third parties. This system is the look and the parts every screen is built from. The wireframes say what is on a screen and in what order. This system says how it looks. Wireframe plus this system is the UI, and the clickable prototype (madison-process-model.vercel.app/prototype/) is that sum for the main journeys.

Using this system

  • Load the fonts from Google Fonts: Onest (400 to 800) for everything, Azeret Mono (400 to 600) for ids, counts and dates. components/bundle.css imports both.
  • Use the CSS variables from tokens.css (one per token below, --you, --space-panel-x, --shadow-lift). Never paste a hex value into a component.
  • components/bundle.css is the component stylesheet. Every class starts md- and reads only tokens. Copy it whole, or port a component's rules into your own components, keeping the values.
  • Build the light theme. A dark theme (Dusk) is drawn for the core colours on the design canvas but not yet for the full palette here.
  • Take the words from the wireframe. This system never adds a word to a screen.

The one rule: what is yours stands out

Every screen answers who has this item now. The answer decides the look.

  • Yours: a raised sheet (sheet, shadow-lift) and the only teal (you) on the screen: the primary button, Needs you, Your task now, the edge on your row.
  • Not yours: a flat sheet (flat with the shadow-ring hairline), no teal.
  • Stopped: a raised sheet ringed in stop (shadow-stop).
  • Madison working: the only thing that moves. Its icon turns counter-clockwise, the way its arrows point.

Choosing is not acting

A chosen option (a segmented control's choice, the All chip, the page you are on in the rail, a checked toggle) is white or a light tint with a ring, and weight 600. It is never teal and never a dark fill, because a fill reads as an action. Teal is kept for the one thing you can do.

Type

One family, Onest, in a short scale, and Azeret Mono for anything a person reads as a code.

  • page-title 24px 700 for a record's name. task-title 22px 800 for your task inside its sheet. card-title 17px 700, section-title 15px 700.
  • body 13px for tables and label and value rows. lead 14px for an action card's line. meta 12px for since lines, header facts and plain links. caption 11px for a step's sub line.
  • column-head and overline: 11px 600, uppercase, letter-spaced 0.08em and 0.1em.
  • Ids (CTL-118, WIR-2026-000015, RUN-2026Q1-CTL118), counts and the dates in a lane are id, id-small or count, in mono.
  • Large type takes negative letter-spacing as set in each style. Headings balance their lines.

Space and layout

  • The page frame: the rail (space-rail 220px), then space-page-left (10px), content, space-page-right (52px, room for the drawer tab). Content starts space-page-top (18px) down and leaves space-page-bottom (96px) under it for the Ask bar.
  • Sheets stack space-panel-gap (14px) apart, padded space-panel-y by space-panel-x (16px by 20px).
  • Every line inside a sheet starts on its 20px text line. A table's outer cells drop their outside padding so the table's text sits on that same line. An inner panel, a pill or an icon may sit on the line, never a few pixels off it.
  • Cards in a grid are space-grid (16px) apart and share their footer line: a card's last row is pushed to its foot.
  • A page grows with its content and scrolls. Nothing is clipped to the window.

Surfaces, depth and radius

  • Radii: radius-sheet 20px for sheets and cards, radius-drawer 22px, radius-inner 16px for a panel inside a sheet, radius-card 14px for chips and small cards, radius-input 12px, radius-pill for buttons and tracks.
  • Depth comes from shadows, never from borders: shadow-lift for yours, the shadow-ring hairline for flat, shadow-drawer for the open drawer, shadow-glow under the primary button. A closed drawer casts no shadow.
  • No coloured left borders on cards. A row that is yours gets a 3px pill in the gutter left of it (see Table).

Tables

Soft dashed lines between cells (grid, 1px dashes on a 10px period), open at every edge: no outline, no line under the last row, none beside the outer columns. Rows have room (space-row-y in a list, 58px on a Home). A row that opens a record is clickable as a whole and ends with a chevron. Text wraps, never ellipsis. No zebra stripes.

Motion

  • Every hover, lift and appear eases with ease-out (cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)) over 160 to 280ms (duration-hover, duration-press, duration-lift, duration-appear). The drawer slides with ease-drawer over duration-drawer.
  • Three things move on their own, and only these: the Madison working icon (one counter-clockwise turn every duration-spin), the dashed ring on Madison's current step (duration-ring), and the pulse on your current step (duration-pulse), with dashes flowing into it (duration-flow).
  • Respect reduced motion: stop all of it.

Focus and contrast

  • Keyboard focus is a 2px solid you outline, 2px out: 5.1:1 on bg, 6.1:1 on sheet.
  • Text pairs meet 4.5:1 except where the notes say: ink-3 on bg is 4.3:1 (keep it on a sheet), stop on stop-soft 4.2:1 and risk on risk-soft 4.4:1 (keep that text 12px semibold or larger). The mark edge (2.4:1) and the line hairline (1.4:1) are below 3:1 for marks: a marked row also says what it is in words, and inputs are found by their label as well as their ring.

Words on screen

  • The wireframe's words, and only those. No helper lines that explain a screen's rule: the rule sits behind an info icon on its count.
  • Sentence case everywhere: Your task now, Worth a look, Ask the bank.
  • A state's icon says the state. Stopped shows the octagon and the reason, never the word Stopped again.
  • Parts of a line are joined by a middle dot: 41 of 45 · 18 days late. A count follows its label: To do 2, New rules 4.
  • People by name and role (Margaret Ellison, Head of Payment Operations). Placeholders keep their brackets ([date], [vendor running WIRE-CORE]).
  • No em dashes, no exclamation marks, no emoji.

Iconography

Stroke icons on a 24px grid, 1.75 stroke with round caps and joins, drawn in the colour of the text or state beside them (currentColor). Most are Lucide's own paths: assets/Icons/README.md names the nearest Lucide icon for each, so lucide-react covers them. Sizes: 16px beside 14px text, 15px in state labels and health, 13px in chips, 11px inside a step dot. Never decorative: an icon names a state, a step or an action.

Not in the system yet

  • A dark theme for the full palette.
  • Charts beyond the plan bar.
  • Message cards (email and Teams nudges) and the pages a vendor or a control owner sees without signing in. The prototype draws them, built from the same tokens.
  • One button height. The prototype has a 40px primary on some task pages: build 38px (size-button).

Button

ActionsOpen preview

The one action that is yours is a teal pill. Everything else is a white pill on a hairline, or a plain link.

Use

  • md-btn: the primary action of a task. One per sheet. It carries shadow-glow.
  • md-btn is-secondary: any other action in the same footer (Return, Redraft, Escalate, Refuse).
  • md-btn is-small is-secondary: a small action beside data (Previous, Next, Seen, Retry now).
  • disabled: the primary while its choices are not made. It turns teal the moment they are (the button waits, it never hides).
  • md-btn is-card is-tint: the button in a Home action card at rest. It fills teal when the card is hovered or focused (see ActionCard).
  • md-link: a way out that changes nothing (Back to the test, Full history).

The consumer provides the label (a verb with its object: Confirm 8 decisions, Sign the plan), an optional 16px icon before it, and the target.

Sizes: size-button 38px is the standard. size-button-card 36px inside cards. size-button-small 30px for small actions. The prototype also has a 40px primary on its round 6 task pages: build 38px.

Do not put two teal buttons in one sheet, use teal for a chosen option (see SegmentedControl), or style a selection as a button.

SegmentedControl

ActionsOpen preview

NavigationOpen preview

Where a record is in its process: one station per step, on one line, under the page header.

Stations

  • Done: a 22px done disc with a check. It opens that step's view-only record (Script, Population, Sample). With no record of its own, it opens the Activity drawer.

  • Now, Madison's: a 32px work-soft disc with a dashed work ring that turns slowly (duration-ring).
  • Now, not yours: a 28px white disc ringed in ink-2.
  • Stopped: a 32px stop disc. Its sub line says what stopped it, in stop.
  • To come: a 22px dashed circle in ink-3, its name in ink-2.

The track: done steps joined by a solid done line, the stretch into the current step in dashes of the current colour that flow toward it (duration-flow, still when stopped), the rest in line.

The consumer provides each step's name, its sub line, its state and, for a finished step, the record it opens. A finished step shows a halo and an underline under the pointer.

MadisonOpen preview

A conversation about the page you are on, docked at the bottom of every page. It replaces the search bar.

Closed: a 560px bar centred under the content, 16px above the window's foot: Madison's mark, Ask Madison about this page, the / key, and send. The page leaves space-page-bottom (96px) under its content so the bar never covers the last row.

Open (click the bar, or press /): the bar widens to 760px and grows to 60% of the window. Its head names the page (the section and the screen). Before a question it offers up to three suggestions drawn from the page (Summarise this page, What needs me here?, What is late or stopped?). Your question sits right, in a soft bubble. Madison's answer sits left beside its mark: one sentence, then the rows or lines it drew on, each a quote that scrolls the page to that place and an Open link to the record. While it reads, the mark line says Reading this page with the working icon. Escape or the chevron closes it.

The consumer provides the answer service. In the prototype the answers are a mock that only quotes the page. The real agent replaces that one function and keeps the answer shape: a sentence, the items it cites, and their targets. Send is grey until there is a question, then teal. A drawer opens above the bar.
